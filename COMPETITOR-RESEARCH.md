## COMPETITOR ANALYSIS: WHAT THEY'RE DOING BADLY

### 1. Weight Watchers (WW) — Major Decline
WW is reportedly preparing for bankruptcy with $1.4 billion+ in debt. (Source: The Guardian, April 2025)

**Key weaknesses:**
- Difficult cancellation and billing complaints
- Inadequate customer support
- Program instability — constant changes to points system
- Shift to GLP-1 drugs alienates core members
- Layoffs of coaches during pandemic

### 2. Noom — Declining from $3.7B Peak
Noom raised $600M+ in VC, peaked at $3.7B valuation in 2021 with $400M+ annual revenue. Now in clear decline.

**Key weaknesses:**
- Bot coaches posing as humans — automated, generic responses
- Content becomes repetitive after 6-8 weeks
- Calorie targets too low (1,200 calories) — can trigger disordered eating
- Tracking technology lagging — no AI photo recognition, no voice logging
- Price keeps increasing — $59-70/month
- Cancellation difficulties

**The gap Noom created:** Users who want psychology-based behavior change with modern tracking technology have NO good option.

### 3. RP Diet — Built for Athletes, Not Everyone
$14.99/month ($119.99/year). Built by strength athletes and PhDs.

**Key weaknesses:**
- Extremely rigid — doesn't work for social dining, travel, or spontaneous eating
- Not a food tracker — can't log what you actually ate
- No micronutrient visibility
- No wearable app integration
- Requires meal prep

**The gap RP creates:** General consumers who want structure without rigidity have no automated option.

### 4. MacroFactor — Best Algorithm, Missing Features
$11.99/month ($71.99/year). Built by Stronger By Science.

**Strength:** Adaptive macro engine that recalibrates weekly based on actual weight trends.

**Key weaknesses:**
- No photo logging — relies entirely on manual search-and-log
- No adaptive coaching for event-based/time-limited goals
- Still designed for indefinite use

### 5. Other Apps (Cronometer, MyFitnessPal, Carbon)
- **Cronometer:** Best micronutrient depth but no adaptive coaching, static targets
- **MyFitnessPal:** Largest database (17M+ entries) but ±18% MAPE accuracy, heavily paywalled
- **Carbon Diet Coach:** Over-specified for competitive bodybuilding, no event features

---

## THE BIGGEST GAP: EVENT-BASED WEIGHT LOSS

**This is the opportunity no major platform is serving well.**

**What ALL competitors miss:**
1. **Time-bound programs with a clear end date** — none say "You're going to this wedding in 12 weeks — here's your program"
2. **Event countdown framing** — psychological urgency of a specific date doesn't exist in any mainstream platform
3. **Photo-based meal evaluation** — most apps don't do this; manual logging is tedious
4. **Real human (or human-sounding) responses** — Noom bots feel fake; human coaches are $200+/month
5. **Transparent timeline** — "10 lbs in 5-10 weeks" sets expectations; competitors are vague

---

## ONE-LINE SUMMARY

No major platform specifically serves time-bound, event-based nutrition coaching with photo evaluation and human-sounding responses — Allen can own this gap.
