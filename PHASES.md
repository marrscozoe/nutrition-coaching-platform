# Nutrition Program Phases — SOURCE OF TRUTH
# Last updated: 2026-08-13

## Phase 1 — Fat Loss (No Starch)
- Protein: 4oz (F) / 6oz (M) per meal
- Veggies: 1-2 cups (F) / 2 cups (M) — fibrous vegetables only
- Fats: 1 tbsp (F) / 2 tbsp (M) — olive oil or 1/2 avocado
- Starch: NO starch — no bread, rice, pasta, potato, beans, corn, oatmeal, cereal
- Water: 80oz (F) / 128oz (M) daily
- Example meal: 6oz grilled chicken breast, 2 cups broccoli, 2 tbsp olive oil, 32oz water

## Phase 2 — Starch Days (Wed/Sat/Sun)
- Protein: 4oz (F) / 6oz (M) per meal
- Veggies: 1-2 cups (F) / 2 cups (M) — fibrous vegetables
- Fats: 1 tbsp (F) / 2 tbsp (M) — olive oil or 1/2 avocado
- Starch: Oatmeal, rice, potato — 1 cup (F) / 2 cups (M) — Wed/Sat/Sun only, first 2 meals only
- NO starch on Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri
- NO starch in dinner or snacks
- Water: 80oz (F) / 128oz (M) daily
- Example meal: 3 eggs scrambled, 2 cups oatmeal, 1 cup spinach, no additional fat, 32oz water

## Phase 3 — Evaluation (Not Used)
- DEPRECATED — Phase 3 is no longer used

## Phase 4 — Maintenance (Starch Every Meal)
- Protein: 4oz (F) / 6oz (M) per meal
- Veggies: 1-2 cups (F) / 2 cups (M) — unlimited fibrous vegetables
- Fats: 1 tbsp (F) / 2 tbsp (M) — olive oil or 1/2 avocado
- Starch: Rice, potato, oatmeal — every meal — 1 cup (F) / 2 cups (M)
- Limit processed starches (bread, chips, fries)
- If weight goes 5+ lbs over goal: back to Phase 1
- Water: 80oz (F) / 128oz (M) daily
- Example meal: 6oz grilled fish, 2 cups rice, 2 cups broccoli, 2 tbsp olive oil, 32oz water

## Phase 5 — Aggressive Fat Loss (Get Shredded)
- Protein: 5oz (F) / 7oz (M) — extra protein for muscle sparing
- Veggies: 1-2 cups (F) / 2 cups (M) — unlimited fibrous vegetables
- Fats: 1 tbsp (F) / 2 tbsp (M) — olive oil, 1/2 avocado
- Starch: NO starch — no rice, potato, bread, pasta, oatmeal, beans, corn
- NO sugar — no fruit, candy, soda, alcohol
- NO processed foods — chips, fries, packaged snacks
- Water: 100oz (F) / 160oz (M) daily — extra hydration for aggressive fat loss
- Example meal: 7oz grilled chicken breast, 3 cups mixed greens, 2 tbsp olive oil, water

## Phase 6 — Muscle Gain (Bulking)
- Protein: 4oz (F) / 6oz (M) per meal — same as other phases
- Veggies: 1-2 cups (F) / 2 cups (M) — fibrous vegetables
- Fats: 2 tbsp (F) / 3 tbsp (M) — olive oil, 1/2 avocado, nuts (HIGHER than other phases)
- Starch: Rice, potato, oatmeal — every meal — 2 cups (F) / 3 cups (M) (HIGHER than other phases)
- Whey protein: 20g × 2/day (F) / 40g × 2/day (M)
- Creatine: Daily
- Limit processed starches (bread, chips, fries)
- If weight goes 5+ lbs over goal: back to Phase 4
- Water: 80oz (F) / 128oz (M) daily
- Example meal: 6oz grilled chicken, 3 cups rice, 2 cups broccoli, 1 tbsp olive oil, 40g whey post-workout, 32oz water

## Program Flows
- **Event Ready:** Phase 1 (14d MAX) → Phase 2 (7d FIXED) → Phase 1 loop OR Phase 4 (goal)
- **Get Shredded:** Phase 1 (14d MAX) → Phase 5 (14d FIXED) → Phase 1 loop OR Phase 4 (goal)
- **General Health:** Starts at Phase 4 — maintenance mode
- **Muscle Gain:** Starts at Phase 6 → Phase 4 (goal attained) → Phase 6 (weight < goal - 5)

## Key Rules
- ALL proteins: 4oz (F) / 6oz (M) per meal — EXCEPT Phase 5 which is 5oz/7oz
- Phase 6 is the ONLY phase with whey protein and creatine
- Phase 6 has higher fat (2-3 tbsp) and starch (2-3 cups) than other phases
- NO dairy while dieting (all phases)
- Bacon: nitrate-free, twice per week max
