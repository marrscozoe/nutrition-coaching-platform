- Medication costs additional: $149-299/month depending on drug (Wegovy, Zepbound, etc.)

**Free Trial:** 14-day "pay what you can" trial ($0.50-$18.37 suggested)

## 10. What Price Points Convert Best?

### Psychology-Based Pricing Observations:

**The "Good-Better-Best" Sweet Spots:**
- **$9.99/$10/month:** Entry-level price point; low barrier to entry; used by WW Core promotional pricing
- **$14.99/month:** Extremely common "value" price point; Macrostax annual, MyFitnessPal annual equivalent; considered "impulse buy" territory
- **$19.99-$24.99/month:** Mid-tier; MyFitnessPal Premium+, standard WW; requires more perceived value
- **$29.99/month:** Popular app price; Ladder monthly, Macrostax monthly; strong conversion when value proposition is clear
- **$39-$49/month:** Premium tier; Noom 4-month equivalent; requires strong differentiation
- **$70+/month:** High-end; monthly Noom; needs substantial perceived value (human coaching, medication access)

**Annual Commitment Discounts:**
- Most apps offer **50-70% off** for annual vs. monthly billing
- This creates a **"hero price" anchor** (e.g., "$17/month vs. $70/month")
- Annual commitments typically convert better when:
  - Free trial reduces risk
  - Cancellation policy is flexible
  - Price is presented as "save X%"

**Conversion-Boosting Strategies Observed:**
1. **Free trial + tiered pricing:** Noom, Ladder, Eat This Much all use this
2. **Promotional pricing:** WW's "$10/month for 12 months" creates urgency
3. **Annual billing as default:** MyFitnessPal and RP Diet push annual with strong discount
4. **"$1 for first month" trials:** Jim Stoppani uses this
5. **Pay-what-you-can trials:** Noom's approach reduces friction

**For New Nutrition Coaching Services, Recommended Starting Points:**
- **$14.99-19.99/month** for automated-only nutrition coaching (competitive with Macrostax)
- **$29.99-39.99/month** for automated + light human support
- **$49-79/month** for full coaching experience
- **Annual discount:** 40-60% off monthly rate (standard industry practice)

| Price Tier | Automation Level | Human Touch | Best For |
|------------|------------------|-------------|----------|
| Free | Basic tracking | None | Casual users |
| $8-15/month | Automated macros/meal plans | None | Self-directed users |
| $17-30/month | Automated coaching + tracking | Optional/lite | Mainstream users |
| $30-50/month | Automated + some human support | Moderate | Engaged users |
| $50-100/month | Human coaching primary | Significant | Committed individuals |
| $100+/month | Full-service coaching | 1-on-1 | Premium/medical clients |

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Noom and WW** dominate the mid-to-upper range with hybrid automated + human coaching models
2. **Macro-tracking apps** (Macrostax, RP Diet) compete on automation and price ($8-30/month)
3. **Human coaching** significantly increases price ($40-100+/month) and retention
4. **Annual commitments** are heavily discounted (50-70%) and represent the primary revenue model
5. **Free trials** (7-14 days) are nearly universal and critical for conversion
6. **$14.99/month** is the most common "value" sweet spot for automated nutrition apps
7. **$29.99/month** is the standard premium app price point
8. **Medication integration (GLP-1)** is becoming a premium upsell across platforms ($70-300+/month)
